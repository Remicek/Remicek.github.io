Install in a minute

  1. Download the appTap Download for Android on your phone. On the GitHub releases page, open the newest version and tap the .apk file.
  2. Open the fileIf Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.
  3. Connect your networksOpen HowYouDoin and pick a network. WhatsApp, Telegram and Signal link like a desktop app; the others log in on their own website inside the app.

Not sure yet? Tap Try demo mode on the first screen to look around with sample chats.

Good to know

HowYouDoin is an unofficial app, not made or supported by the networks it connects. Some of them don't allow third-party apps and may limit or block an account that uses one, so the app warns you before each connection.

Voice and video calls work with Matrix contacts. Google Messages and Google Voice aren't available: Google blocks signing in from apps like this one.
